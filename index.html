Scientist and software engineer in one
I build a bioinformatics platform with research-grade rigor

I hold a PhD in health biology (genomics and bioinformatics) and spent 10 years between academic research and startups all over the globe (Italy, France, UK, USA) working on cancer genomics, immunogenomics, developmental biology and developing bioinformatics tools published in top journals.

Full-stack bioinformatics systems engineered with the same rigor as the research behind them, running on private HPCs and cloud infrastructure (AWS). FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) disciple.
