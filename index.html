About

I hold a PhD in computational biology and spent 8+ years in academic research — cancer genomics, developmental biology, and bioinformatics tools published in top journals.

I don't just write analysis scripts — I build platforms. Full-stack bioinformatics systems that turn large-scale biological data into tools scientists actually use: precision oncology, mitochondrial dynamics, synthetic plasmid scoring. Engineered with the same rigor as the research behind them, running on private HPCs and cloud infrastructure (AWS).

Lead bioinformatician, platform developer and scientific advisor at Allovista (US) and SeCore (UK), translating research-grade methods into production-ready platforms.

Also freelancing now — basically, call me if you get lost. P.IVA 02856570037
